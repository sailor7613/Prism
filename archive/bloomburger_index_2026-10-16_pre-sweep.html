BloomBurger · the paperParallelogram

PGSSTWIREROSTER
The day's discourse, reduced. Each parallelogram is one segment on one object, cut to the words that carry it, scored, and placed on the graphmap — for the reader who is tired of the news but wants to be in the know from a distance.
A parallelogram's figure is literal: join the four stations' composite readings and you get a four-sided shape whose skew is the segment's split. Dia is distance from the object; z is what the voice believes happened; Lumens is how much of the object's light gets through.
