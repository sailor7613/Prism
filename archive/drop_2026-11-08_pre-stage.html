What happens

  1. Grammar. The posts are split into lines: one statement, one speaker; what each is about (the object, or the shadow: who's to blame), and the few words that carry it.
  2. Context. Every line is read against the tracker it talks about, as of its own date.
  3. The relational test. Does the line take up the object or a signal about it; is it open, working, or closed toward the other side; is capital spent. The rules turn the answers into a Diatribe number.
  4. Bands. Four stations × three tiers, each with its own z.
  5. The second pass. Where a speaker's own earlier words come due and he refuses them, or takes up what he had denied.
  6. The cloud. The lit words on the graphmap, each tied to its line.

Rules it keeps

  • Nothing is censored: posts go in as posted.
  • Shadow lines carry no z; they read "unmeasured."
  • The grammar is saved: the same set gives the same lines every time.
  • Sailor's rulings are shown to the model as settled cases.
