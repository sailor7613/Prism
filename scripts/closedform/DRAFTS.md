## What each draft taught

- **Draft 1.** Fixing the cut separates how a model reads from how clear the question is. The band is loose because one question under it is loose. DeepSeek disagrees with itself too much to be counted.
- **Draft 2.** A ruling written into the question, with its line as the example, brings all four readers to the same answer on that line. The Crystal did not move: its splits were a different kind.
- **Draft 3.** Rewording alone did not tighten the band. Reading each reader's reasons showed why: the pattern library steers Qwen, the question showed today's Object to old lines, and a three-way choice gives Gemma a middle to retreat to.
- **Draft 4.** *Predicted (written before the result was read):* it worked if the three agree on the band for more than 55% of Tucker's lines, failed under 50%. *Happened:* 67% on Tucker, 72% on the Crystal. It worked. The verdict question holds (83% · 86%); the reason question is now the loose one (67% · 58%). Each reader's own spread on Tucker fell to about 3 points. Four things changed at once, so which one did it is not known.
  *Not fixed:* the Dia number itself. The three land within 5 points of each other on 17% of Tucker's lines and 36% of the Crystal's. Even where they agree on the band, they place the line differently inside it (middle gap 8.5 and 6.5 points). The answers that set the place are loose: substance or signal (60% · 75%), facing (55% · 72%), and the patterns (Sonnet 3 lines, Qwen 8, Gemma 0 on Tucker).
  DeepSeek, tracked: its own spread is down to 13 and 10 points (from 38 and 34 at draft 1); it matches the majority's band on 68% and 77% of lines.

## After draft 4

The working set is closed for drafting. The next set is new discourse the rules have not seen, read under draft 4 as it stands. How far agreement falls on a new set is the measure of how much of the working set's agreement was fitting. The working set is then re-read under each later draft only as a check that nothing got worse.

## Rulings, 2026-11-16 (evening)

Sailor ruled six kinds of line; all are in `rulings.json` (principles and notForecast), none yet in the questions. For draft 5: a fact held up as a complaint leans frustrated; a claim to be right, a hope, a wait-and-see are rhetorical, not forecasts; the band of a prayer comes from what it says about others; a question is a question unless the post answers it; the speaker on himself is shadow; a teaser is the whole post (the readers missed the 2026-10-23 one).
