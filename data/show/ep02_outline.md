# The Parallelogram Report — Episode Two · outline v1
*2026-10-30 · Sailor + Claude. Target 15–20 min. Airs after the 11-03 election. Two objects, linked: a bill with no money behind it doing what it says, and money with no bill behind it at all.*

**Working title:** Episode Two · Who Gets Paid

## The two objects
1. **Half a Ban — H.R. 7008, the Stop Insider Trading Act** (legislative object). Bans members of Congress from buying new stock; existing holdings kept; $2,000 fine. Passed the House 07-22 joined to the Voter ID Act; failed to advance in the Senate 53–47 on 09-30. Readings on file: `rdg_2cs16s93dghl` "Half a Ban", `rdg_l9xd9omvnpa7` "Seven Short" (duplicates — merge to one before the episode).
2. **Trump's Midterm Bribe — the $5,000 dividend** (discourse subject, legislative void). Dallas, 09-09: every adult citizen gets $5,000 if Republicans hold the House and Senate. Moreno (09-10): a bill after the election, paid for by "market access fees" on foreign goods. No bill filed. CRFB: ~$1.2T. Thune: "a reconciliation issue." Earlier rebate bills that never moved: Hawley's American Worker Rebate Act (2025, $600), Heinrich's. Reading on file: "The Five-Thousand-Dollar If" (live; its title in the app unchanged).

## The link (what the episode is about)
**The tariff.** The dividend is tariff money paid out to voters. The stock ban got its biggest push from tariff money moving the other way: members trading around the April 2025 tariff announcements and the pause (AOC and others asking for investigations; Fortune's count of members' trades around "Liberation Day"). Same instrument, two questions:
- Who gets paid when the government moves the market? (7008: members, from what they know before you do.)
- Who gets paid when the government collects from the market? (The dividend: you, if you vote the right way.)
Second link, functional: **both were tied to the election.** 7008 was carried with an election rule (voter ID) attached, which is what sank it. The dividend is conditioned on an election result. In both, a thing almost everyone says they want is tied to something that splits them.

## Election-day hinge
The dividend's condition resolves 11-03. The segment is written after the result:
- GOP holds both → Moreno's bill becomes a real object for the lame duck/reconciliation; the ledger tracks it.
- GOP loses either → the promise lapses unfunded; the ledger closes it as an object that never formed.
7008 is a lame-duck object either way (and new members ran on a ban).
